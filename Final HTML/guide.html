TRAVEL SMART

Passenger Guide

Helpful reminders for passengers using jeepneys around Cagayan de Oro City.

Before You Ride

Prepare before boarding so your journey can be more convenient.

01

Check the Route Sign

Read the jeepney's route sign and check whether it includes your intended destination or area.

02

Ask Before Boarding

If you are unsure about the route, ask the driver or conductor whether the jeepney passes your destination.

03

Prepare Your Fare

Keep your fare ready when possible. Ask about the current fare if you are uncertain.

04

Keep Your Belongings Secure

Keep your phone, wallet, bag, and other personal belongings secure while traveling.

05

Be Respectful to Other Passengers

Give others enough space, follow the driver's instructions, and be considerate of elderly passengers and persons with disabilities.

06

Confirm Your Drop-Off Point

Tell the driver or conductor where you need to get off, and use a safe, permitted stopping point.

TRAVEL CHECKLIST

Before Leaving Home

Remember these basic travel preparations.

  • Know your destination.
  • Check the route sign.
  • Bring enough fare.
  • Keep your phone and belongings secure.
  • Allow extra time for traffic.
  • Confirm route details if uncertain.
