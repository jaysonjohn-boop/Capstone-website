Popular Destinations

Learn about some common landmarks and travel destinations in Cagayan de Oro City.

Travel reminder: The destinations below are examples, not a guarantee that every listed jeepney route passes directly through each location. Confirm the route with the driver or conductor before boarding.
01

Cogon Public Market

A busy public market area where people shop for food, household goods, and other daily necessities.

Destination type: Public market

02

Carmen Public Market

A local market destination serving residents and shoppers in the Carmen area.

Destination type: Public market

03

Agora Market and Terminal

A market and transport area used by passengers traveling to and from different parts of the city.

Destination type: Market and terminal

04

Divisoria

A well-known central area with shops, businesses, and places for people to meet and run errands.

Destination type: Commercial area

05

Puerto

An area in Cagayan de Oro that serves as a destination for local commuters and nearby communities.

Destination type: Community area

06

City Center

The central part of the city, where passengers may find commercial establishments and other services.

Destination type: Urban center
